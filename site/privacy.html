Keyholder

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Keyholder helps you put your phone down. You block apps, and only a friend who holds your key can give you time back. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to remove it.

The short version: Keyholder never learns which apps you block, never sees your app usage, and has no ads or tracking. Your keyholder sees your name, your streak and your requests for time, nothing more.

What Keyholder stores

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier for your Apple account and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.
  • Your display name, which your keyholder and the people whose keys you hold can see.
  • Keys and invites. Who holds whose key, and the invite codes you create.
  • Blocks. When a block starts and ends, the cooling-off length you chose, and how many apps and categories it covers. Only the counts: we do not know which apps they are.
  • Requests and decisions. When you ask for time, how much you asked for, and what your keyholder decided.
  • Your streak and record, calculated from the above.
  • Device information. A push notification token so we can notify you, a random secret that lets the app's Screen Time extensions talk to our server, and the time your phone last checked in.
  • Notification history, the text of notifications we send you.

What Keyholder does not collect

  • Which apps you block. Apple's Screen Time system keeps your selection on your phone as private tokens that Keyholder cannot read or send anywhere.
  • Your app usage, screen time totals, browsing, messages, contacts, photos or location.
  • Any advertising identifiers. Keyholder does not track you across apps or websites and shows no ads.

Who can see your data

  • Your keyholder sees your display name, whether you are locked, your streak and record, the number of apps and categories you blocked, your requests for time, and whether you have started Break Glass.
  • People whose key you hold see your display name.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase hosts the database and server in the European Union (Ireland), and Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications. They process data only to provide these services.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When your account is deleted, your profile, keys, blocks, requests, streaks, devices and notifications are deleted with it.

Deleting your data

During the beta, you can ask for your account and all its data to be deleted by sending feedback through TestFlight: take a screenshot in Keyholder and choose Share Beta Feedback, or open Keyholder in the TestFlight app and tap Send Beta Feedback. In-app account deletion is coming in a later build.

You can also stop Keyholder at any time by turning off its Screen Time access in iOS Settings, which ends any active block.

Children

Keyholder is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
